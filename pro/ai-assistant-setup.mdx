## What you're turning on

The Coterie Assistant is a built-in AI helper members can ask about their own credits, assignments, and upcoming sessions — see [Coterie Assistant](/members/coterie-assistant) for what it looks like from a member's side. As the creator, you're the one who turns it on and controls how it's used.

## Setting it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring your own OpenAI key" icon="key">
    The assistant runs on a key you supply — it's disabled by default until you configure one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a daily message cap" icon="sliders">
    Control usage per member per day, so you have a predictable ceiling on cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on" icon="toggle-right">
    Once configured, the assistant becomes available to your members immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it can and can't see

The assistant only ever answers from a member's own data within your Coterie — their own posts, assignments, and credit history. It doesn't see other members' activity, and it isn't a general-purpose chatbot answering questions unrelated to your community.

<Callout kind="tip">
  This is a genuinely differentiated feature for a community your size — most engagement groups don't have anything like it. Turning it on is a low-effort way to make your Coterie feel more polished than the free-form group chats members are used to.
</Callout>
