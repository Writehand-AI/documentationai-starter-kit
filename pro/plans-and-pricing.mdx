## What plans are for

Not every Coterie needs to charge for access, but plenty benefit from it — a paid tier signals commitment, filters for members who are serious, and gives you a way to gate premium content like [Resources](/members/resources) behind a level of investment.

## Defining a plan

From your admin dashboard, you can define paid tiers with:

- A name and description
- A price and billing interval
- The features or access included at that level

You can run multiple tiers side by side — a free tier for general members and a paid tier that unlocks deeper resources, priority matching, or whatever else makes sense for your community.

<Callout kind="warning">
  Live checkout isn't connected yet — you can fully define your plan structure today, but members can't pay through the app until billing goes live. Plan the tiers now so you're ready the moment it does.
</Callout>

## Structuring tiers well

The strongest plan structures map to something a member can clearly feel the difference of — more resources, better matching priority, access to a smaller inner circle — not just a paywall on the same experience everyone else already gets for free.
