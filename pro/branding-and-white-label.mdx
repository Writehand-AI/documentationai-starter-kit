## Why this matters

Members trust things that look like they belong to someone. A community that's visibly "yours" — your logo, your colors, your voice — reads as a real, considered thing you built for them. A community that looks like a generic template reads as a side project. White-labeling isn't cosmetic; it's part of why people take a community seriously enough to actually show up.

## What you can brand

<Steps>
  <Step title="Logo" icon="image">
    Upload your own logo, shown throughout your Coterie's app and public pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accent color" icon="palette">
    Set the color that carries through buttons, highlights, and key UI — your brand, not Coterie's default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Theme" icon="sun">
    Fine-tune the overall look so your community's app feels consistent with the rest of your brand presence.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where it shows up

Branding applies across your entire member experience — the app itself, your public apply page at `coterie.rsvp/c/your-slug`, and system emails (see [Email Customization](/pro/email-customization) for the copy side of that). It's not a cosmetic skin on one page; it's consistent everywhere a member or applicant interacts with your community.

## Setting it up

From your admin dashboard, open Branding and upload your logo and set your accent color. Changes apply immediately — there's no review or approval step, so you can iterate until it looks right.
