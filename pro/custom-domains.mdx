## Why it's worth doing

There's a real difference between sending someone to `app.coterie.rsvp/app/your-community` and sending them to `community.yourbrand.com`. One says "I'm using a tool." The other says "this is mine." For creators building a serious community around their brand, a custom domain is what makes the whole thing feel native instead of borrowed.

## What it does

A custom domain maps your own domain (or subdomain) straight to your Coterie — your members and applicants never see `coterie.rsvp` at all once it's set up. Everything else about the platform works exactly the same underneath; this is purely about the address your community lives at.

## Setting one up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your domain or subdomain" icon="globe">
    Decide what you want members to type — a root domain or a subdomain like `community.yourbrand.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point your DNS" icon="settings">
    From your admin dashboard's domain settings, you'll get the records to add at your DNS provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify" icon="check-circle">
    Once your DNS change propagates, your domain gets verified and starts serving your Coterie directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  DNS changes can take time to propagate depending on your provider — don't worry if it's not instant.
</Callout>

## Pair it with branding

A custom domain on its own is a URL change. A custom domain paired with your [logo and colors](/pro/branding-and-white-label) is a fully white-labeled community — worth doing both together if you're going to do either.
