## Recent Updates

<Callout kind="info">
  Coterie is early and moving fast. This log covers the most recent, user-facing changes.
</Callout>

<Update label="2026-08-18" description="Meet the Coterie Assistant" tags={["feature"]}>

- **Coterie Assistant** — every community can now turn on a built-in AI assistant that members can ask about their credits, assignments, and upcoming sessions, right from the app.
- **Faster, better-looking emails** — invites, session reminders, and application updates now arrive quicker and read cleaner.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-16" description="Discover, onboarding, and custom links" tags={["feature"]}>

- **Discover** — browse public Coteries and apply to join, right from inside the app.
- **Guided onboarding** — new members now pick their niche and get oriented in a few quick steps.
- **Custom sidebar links** — creators can add their own links for members, opening in a new tab or right inside the app.
- **Custom application questions** — creators can ask applicants exactly what they need to know before approving them.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-15" description="Cleaner navigation, new Admin Hub" tags={["improvement"]}>

- **Simplified navigation** — everything is easier to find, with less digging through menus.
- **Admin Hub** — a single home base for creators to manage their community.
- Behind-the-scenes hardening to keep member data locked down.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-08-14" description="Custom domains &amp; white-label branding" tags={["feature"]}>

- **Custom domains** — run your Coterie under your own domain instead of a shared one.
- **White-label branding** — your logo and colors now carry through the entire member experience.

</Update>
