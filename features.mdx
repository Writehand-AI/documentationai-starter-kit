## Overview

Coterie is built around one mechanic: **give-to-get reciprocity**, run in scheduled sessions instead of an open-ended feed. Members submit content, get matched with others to engage with, and earn credit for following through. Everything else — niches, resources, the AI assistant, admin tools — supports that loop.

## Parties

A **party** is a scheduled engagement session with a time window, a maximum number of participants, and a room size. Admins schedule parties from the admin dashboard; members opt in from their **Sessions** calendar.

When a party runs:

1. Members submit a link to a post they want engagement on.
2. Coterie auto-groups participants into rooms and assigns each member a set of other members' posts.
3. Members complete their assignments before the window closes.
4. The party moves through `scheduled → grouping → running → finished`.

## Assignments and credits

An **assignment** is the pairing of "this member" with "that post." Each one tracks status, any rating or feedback left, a due date, and an optional skip reason.

Credits are the currency that keeps reciprocity fair:

- Completing an assignment earns credit.
- Submitting a new post costs credit — if a member has outstanding "comment debt" (assignments they haven't completed), they can't submit again until they catch up.
- Every credit movement is recorded in an append-only ledger, so a member's balance is always auditable.

Alongside credits, each member has a **reliability score** that reflects how consistently they follow through, plus a strike count for moderation. Members who fall behind can be paused; repeated no-shows accrue strikes.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Comment debt is what makes this different from a normal engagement pod — you can't just keep posting without giving back.
</Callout>

## Niches

Every member belongs to one **niche** — the industry or content category they operate in (this is set during onboarding). Assignments and, where enabled, competition-exclusion rules use niche to keep matches relevant and avoid pairing direct competitors.

## Moderation and reporting

Members can **report** a post or an assignment if something's off. Reports go into a queue admins review from the admin dashboard, alongside the strike system for members who repeatedly skip assignments or misbehave.

## Discover

The **Discover** page lists public Coteries across the platform that members can browse and apply to join — separate from any single community's own apply page, and not scoped to a particular tenant.

## Resources

Creators can publish **resources** — training or guide content, optionally gated by plan tier — for their members. These live inside the member app alongside a creator's other custom content.

## Wins

Members can log a **win** — a self-reported success story (a post that did well, a deal that closed, etc.) that shows up on their dashboard. It's a lightweight way to make the value of showing up visible.

## Coterie Assistant

Each community can turn on its own AI assistant — a chatbot scoped to a member's own data (their posts, assignments, credit history). It runs on a creator-supplied OpenAI key (bring-your-own-key), with a configurable daily message cap.

## Admin and creator tools

Everything below is available to a Coterie's admin from their admin dashboard:

  <Step title="Applications" icon="inbox">
    Review and approve or reject people applying to join through your public apply page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Parties" icon="calendar">
    Create and schedule sessions, and manage grouping/room sizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settings" icon="sliders">
    Configure allowed content channels, default party size and window, rotation rules, and strike thresholds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Branding &amp; white-label" icon="palette">
    Set your logo, accent color, and theme. Custom domains are supported in the product, letting a community run under its own domain rather than an `app.coterie.rsvp` URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email" icon="mail">
    Override system email copy per-tenant, or configure your own SMTP sender instead of the platform default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plans" icon="tag">
    Define paid access tiers (name, price, interval, features) for your community. Plan definitions exist today, but there's no live checkout yet — see [Integrations](/integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Custom menu" icon="menu">
    Add your own links to the member sidebar, opening in a new tab or embedded in-app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Community health" icon="activity">
    A view of aggregate engagement and churn-risk signals across your community.
  </Step>
</Steps>
