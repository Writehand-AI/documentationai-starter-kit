## Welcome

Coterie is a platform for running small, invite-vetted professional communities built around showing up for each other's work. Members share something they've published — a post, article, newsletter, design, video or any other link — into a scheduled session. Coterie then helps the group discover and review each other's work in a structured, relevant way.

What members do after discovering someone's work is their choice. They might leave feedback, start a conversation, support it on the original platform, or simply read it and move on. Coterie structures participation, not external engagement.

You don't need everyone. You just need your people. That's a Coterie.

## The problem with growing an audience alone

Publishing consistently is one thing. Building a genuine circle of people who actually read, understand and respond to your work is harder.

Most creators either publish into a broad public feed and hope the right people find them, or join noisy group chats where links are dropped without much context or genuine interest.

Neither creates what most professionals are really looking for: a trusted group of relevant people who know their work, give useful feedback, share ideas and consistently show up for one another.

## Why Coterie is different

Coterie replaces goodwill with structure. Three things make the difference:

**Relevant circles instead of a firehose.** Members belong to niches so Coterie can surface work from people whose ideas, experience and context are likely to be relevant.

**Scheduled sessions instead of another endless feed.** Sessions give communities a regular rhythm for sharing work and discovering what other members are creating.

**Participation instead of passive membership.** Coterie recognises members who consistently show up, review work and contribute to their community. Participation credits keep the community active without requiring any particular action on an external platform.

## What this means for you

If you're building an audience around your expertise, Coterie gives your work a consistent starting audience of relevant people who have chosen to be part of your circle — people who can read it, challenge it, discuss it and support it when it genuinely resonates.

If you're building a community around an audience or niche, Coterie gives you the infrastructure to run it: applications, member matching, sessions, participation, moderation and white-label branding — without turning your community into another noisy feed or group chat.
