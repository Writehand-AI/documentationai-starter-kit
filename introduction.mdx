## Welcome

Coterie is a platform for running invite-vetted communities where members give each other real, reciprocal engagement on the content they create — LinkedIn posts, blog posts, newsletters, designs, and more. Members submit a link into a scheduled session, get automatically matched with other members' posts to comment on, and earn credit for showing up. No bots, no engagement pods scraping for likes — just people in your niche actually reading and responding to your work.

You don't need everyone. You just need your people. That's a Coterie.

Each community is its own **Coterie** — a self-contained, brandable workspace that a creator runs for their audience. Coterie itself is the platform those communities run on.

## How the loop works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit a post" icon="link">
    A member shares a link to something they've published (a LinkedIn post, blog article, etc.) into an open party.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get assigned" icon="shuffle">
    Coterie automatically assigns each member a set of other members' posts to engage with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engage" icon="message-circle">
    Members leave real comments or feedback on their assigned posts before the party window closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn credit" icon="star">
    Completing assignments earns credit and builds a member's reliability score. Members with outstanding "comment debt" can't submit a new post until they catch up — that's what keeps the reciprocity honest.
  </Step>
</Steps>
