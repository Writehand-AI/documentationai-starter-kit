## Welcome

Coterie is a platform for running invite-vetted communities built on one idea: engagement should be earned by giving it, not bought or begged for. Members submit their content — a LinkedIn post, a blog article, a newsletter, a design — into a scheduled session, get matched with other members' work to comment on in return, and earn credit for showing up. It's structured, accountable reciprocity, not a feed.

You don't need everyone. You just need your people. That's a Coterie.

## The problem with growing an audience alone

Publishing consistently is the easy part. Getting anyone to actually respond is the hard part.

Most creators end up in one of two places. Either they post into silence — the algorithm doesn't show new or small accounts to anyone, so early posts get no signal, which means the algorithm shows them to even fewer people next time. Or they join an "engagement pod" — a Discord or WhatsApp group where everyone drops links and everyone else drive-by likes them. It works for about two weeks. Then the group goes quiet, half the comments are copy-pasted filler, and nobody's actually reading anything.

Neither gets you what you're really after: real people, in your niche, engaging with your work because it's genuinely relevant to them — not because they're feeling generous that day.

## Why Coterie is different

Coterie replaces goodwill with structure. Three things make the difference:

**Small, matched circles instead of a firehose.** Every member belongs to a niche. Assignments are built from people who actually operate in your space, so the feedback you get is relevant, not random.

**Scheduled sessions instead of an open feed.** Engagement happens in time-boxed "parties," not an endless stream nobody commits to. When a party's open, there's a clear window to submit and a clear window to engage.

**Credit instead of goodwill.** Every member earns credit by completing assignments and spends it by submitting new posts. If you owe engagement from a previous round, you can't submit again until you've paid it off. That single rule is what keeps a Coterie from decaying into the same dead group chat everyone's already been in.

## What this means for you

If you're a creator trying to build an audience, Coterie gives you a standing source of real, relevant engagement every time you publish — the kind that actually moves the algorithm and actually reads as social proof to anyone else who lands on your post.

If you're building a community around your own audience or niche, Coterie gives you the infrastructure to run it: applications, scheduling, moderation, and — if you want your own brand front and center — white-label branding and a custom domain, so it's your community, not a feature inside someone else's app.
