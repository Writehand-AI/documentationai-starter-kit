## What you're submitting

A submission is a link to something you've already published — a LinkedIn post, a blog article, an Instagram post, whatever channels your Coterie allows. Coterie doesn't post on your behalf; you publish it yourself, then bring the link in so other members can find and engage with it.

## When you can submit

You can only submit into an **open party** — a scheduled session your community's admin has running. If none is open, check your Sessions calendar for the next one.

<Callout kind="warning">
  If you still owe engagement from a previous round — unfinished assignments, also called "comment debt" — you can't submit a new post until you've caught up. This is the rule that keeps the whole system fair: you don't get to keep taking without giving back.
</Callout>

## Submitting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Submit" icon="link">
    From your dashboard, go to Submit — it's only active while a party is open and you're clear of comment debt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your link" icon="clipboard">
    Add the URL and pick the channel it's on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm" icon="check-circle">
    Once submitted, your post enters the pool other members will be matched against.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you submit

Once the party moves into grouping, Coterie assigns your post to other members to engage with — and assigns you posts from others in return. You can track your submission's status and see the engagement it received from **Posts**, including per-post detail once assignments are complete.

<Callout kind="tip">
  A submission with real context — what you're asking for, what kind of feedback would actually help — gets better engagement than a bare link. Members are matched to your post either way, but what they do with it is still up to them.
</Callout>
