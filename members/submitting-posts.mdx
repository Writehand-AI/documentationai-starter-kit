## What you're submitting

A submission is a link to something you've already published — a LinkedIn post, a blog article, an Instagram post, whatever channels your Coterie allows. Coterie doesn't post on your behalf. You publish your work yourself, then share the link with your Coterie so relevant members of your circle can discover and review it.

## When you can submit

You can only submit into an **open party** — a scheduled session your community's owner has running. If none is open, check your Sessions calendar for the next one.

<Callout kind="warning">
  If you have outstanding reviews from a session you joined previously, you may need to complete or resolve those before submitting again. This keeps participation balanced without requiring any particular external-platform action.
</Callout>

## Submitting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Submit" icon="link">
    From your dashboard, go to Submit — it's only active while a party is open and you're clear of outstanding reviews.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your link" icon="clipboard">
    Add the URL and pick the channel it's on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm" icon="check-circle">
    Once submitted, your post enters the pool other members will be matched against.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you submit

Once the session moves into matching, Coterie surfaces your submission to relevant members and gives you work from others to review. What anyone chooses to do on the original platform remains their decision. You can track your submission's status and see the participation it received from **Posts**, including per-post detail once reviews are complete.

<Callout kind="tip">
  A submission with real context — what you're asking for, what kind of feedback would actually help — tends to get more thoughtful reviews than a bare link. Members are matched to your post either way, but what they do with it is still up to them.
</Callout>
