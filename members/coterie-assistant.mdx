## What it does

The Coterie Assistant is a built-in AI helper, scoped to your own activity in the community. Ask it things like "how many reviews do I have outstanding," "when's my next party," or "what have I earned this month" and it answers from your actual data — no digging through tabs to find it yourself.

## Help with your comments

If your creator has it switched on, the assistant also shows the posts you owe comments on. Open the list from the top of the message box, and pick one to hand it to the assistant — it fills the box with the post's title, whatever the author asked for, and the author's own text if they shared it. You can edit all of that before you send it.

It's there to help you work out what's worth saying, not to save you reading the post. The comment lands with a real person who wrote something, and they can tell.

Where the author didn't paste their text, the assistant will say so and ask you to add it, rather than writing something from the title alone.

## How many messages you get

Some Coteries set a monthly limit, because the assistant runs on the creator's own AI account and costs them money per message. You won't see anything about it until you're close — then a line under the message box tells you how many you have left.

Limits run on the calendar month, so they reset on the 1st. If a plan in your Coterie includes more than you have, the message you get when you run out will say so.

## Availability

Not every Coterie has this turned on — it's opt-in for creators, and only available where they've enabled it. If you don't see it in your app, your community hasn't set it up yet.

<Callout kind="info">
  The assistant only ever answers about your own activity within your Coterie. It's not a general-purpose chatbot, and it isn't looking at other members' data.
</Callout>
