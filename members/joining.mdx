## Find the right one

Every Coterie is built around a niche and a standard, set by whoever created it. That's the point — you're not joining a generic engagement group, you're joining people who actually work in your space. Find one two ways:

- **Discover** — browse public Coteries from inside the app and apply to the ones that fit.
- **A direct invite** — a creator or existing member shares their community's page with you directly, something like `coterie.rsvp/c/their-community`.

## Apply

Every Coterie has a public apply page at `coterie.rsvp/c/:slug`. You'll give your name and email, and answer whatever questions the creator's added — these usually cover what you post, how often, and what you're hoping to get out of the group. Take them seriously: they're often the only signal the admin has before deciding whether you're a fit.

<Callout kind="info">
  Some communities auto-approve applications for instant access. Others are reviewed by an admin — approval time depends entirely on them, not on Coterie.
</Callout>

## Get approved and set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for approval" icon="clock">
    If the community isn't auto-approve, the admin reviews your application against their standard. You'll get an invite by email once you're in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
    Sign up at [app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp) using the same email you applied with — password, Google, or GitHub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding" icon="compass">
    Pick your niche and accept the community's rules. Your niche is what determines whose posts you'll be matched with going forward, so pick the one that actually describes what you do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you're through onboarding, you land on your dashboard — your credit balance, reliability score, and the next open party are all right there. From here, the next step is [submitting your first post](/members/submitting-posts).
