## Credits

Participation credits help balance sharing and contribution inside a Coterie. Members use credit when submitting work and earn it by completing genuine reviews within the community.

Credits measure participation inside Coterie. They are not earned for likes, comments, shares, follows or any other action on an external platform.

The rule that makes this work: if you have outstanding reviews from a previous round, you can't submit a new post until you've completed or resolved them. That's the entire mechanism that keeps a Coterie from decaying into people posting into a void while nobody participates back.

## Reliability score

Separate from your credit balance, your **reliability score** reflects whether a member consistently participates in the community sessions they choose to join — not just whether you eventually get to a review, but whether you show up on time, round after round. It's the signal your community's owner (and, in some communities, other members) uses to judge whether you're someone worth being matched with.

## Strikes

Strikes exist for repeatedly failing to participate after opting into sessions, spam, abuse, inappropriate submissions, dishonest completion, or misuse of the community — not for choosing to skip a match that genuinely isn't relevant. Enough strikes, and an owner can pause your membership — this protects the group's quality for everyone else, not just the person who fell behind.

<Callout kind="tip">
  If you know you're going to miss a window — travel, a busy week, whatever — skip the review with a reason rather than letting it silently lapse. It's not free, but it's better for your reliability than ghosting.
</Callout>

## Where to check yours

Your dashboard shows your current credit balance, reliability score, and any outstanding reviews at a glance — it's the first thing worth checking before you try to submit.
