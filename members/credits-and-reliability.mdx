## Credits

Credit is what you spend to submit a post, and what you earn by completing assignments. Every movement — earned or spent — is recorded in an append-only ledger, so your balance is always traceable back to exactly what you did to get there.

The rule that makes this work: if you owe engagement from a previous round (comment debt), you can't submit a new post until you've paid it down. That's the entire mechanism that keeps a Coterie from decaying into people posting into a void while nobody engages back.

## Reliability score

Separate from your credit balance, your **reliability score** reflects how consistently you actually follow through on what you're assigned — not just whether you eventually get to it, but whether you show up on time, round after round. It's the signal your community's admin (and, in some communities, other members) uses to judge whether you're someone worth being matched with.

## Strikes

Members who repeatedly skip assignments or misuse the system accrue **strikes**. Enough strikes, and an admin can pause your membership — this protects the group's quality for everyone else, not just the person who fell behind.

<Callout kind="tip">
  If you know you're going to miss a window — travel, a busy week, whatever — skip the assignment with a reason rather than letting it silently lapse. It's not free, but it's better for your reliability than ghosting.
</Callout>

## Where to check yours

Your dashboard shows your current credit balance, reliability score, and any open comment debt at a glance — it's the first thing worth checking before you try to submit.
