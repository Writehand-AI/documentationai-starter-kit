## What an assignment is

An assignment pairs you with someone else's post and asks you to genuinely engage with it — a real comment, real feedback, whatever your community's format calls for. It's the other half of the loop: you submitted your own work expecting real engagement, so this is you providing it for someone else.

Each assignment tracks:

- The post you've been matched with
- A due date, tied to the party's window
- Your rating or feedback once you've completed it
- A skip reason, if you genuinely can't get to it

## How matching works

Assignments are built automatically when a party moves into grouping, matched primarily within your niche so the pairing is actually relevant — you shouldn't be stuck leaving feedback on something you don't understand, and someone in your niche shouldn't be graded by a stranger who is. Where a creator enables competition-exclusion, you also won't be matched with people they've flagged as direct competitors.

## Working through your assignments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Assignments" icon="inbox">
    Your assigned posts show up in **Assignments**, with their due dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Engage properly" icon="message-circle">
    Read the post, leave real comment or feedback — copy-pasted filler defeats the entire point of the system, and your community's admin can see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it done" icon="check-circle">
    Complete the assignment once you've engaged. This is what releases the credit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report if something's wrong" icon="flag">
    If a post is inappropriate or doesn't belong, report it instead of engaging — it routes to your community's moderation queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why this matters more than it looks like

Every assignment you skip becomes comment debt, and debt blocks your next submission. So this isn't a nice-to-have side quest — it's the mechanism that earns you the engagement you're actually here for.
