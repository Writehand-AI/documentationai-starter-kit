## What a niche is

A niche is the industry or content category you operate in — marketing, SaaS, design, coaching, whatever your community defines. You pick yours during onboarding, and it's the single biggest factor in who you get matched with.

## Why it matters

Random matching is what makes generic engagement pods useless — you end up leaving comments on things you don't understand, and getting comments back from people who don't understand yours. Niche matching fixes that at the source: assignments are built primarily from within your niche, so the person reading your post actually has context for it, and the feedback you give back is worth something.

## Competition-exclusion

Some communities enable an extra layer on top of niche matching: excluding direct competitors from your assignment pool. If you're a copywriter and so is the person your Coterie would otherwise match you with, this keeps you from quietly reviewing each other's client pitches. Not every community turns this on — check with your admin if you're not sure whether yours does.

<Callout kind="info">
  You belong to exactly one niche at a time. If your work has genuinely shifted, ask your community's admin about changing it — this isn't something members can self-serve.
</Callout>
