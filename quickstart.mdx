## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

<Callout kind="info">

- A valid email address for account verification
- Access to a modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- A few minutes to set up your first circle

No credit card required to get started.

</Callout>

## Create Your First Coterie in 4 Steps

Follow these steps to sign up, build your circle, invite members, and launch your first support session. You'll be up and running in under 5 minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up and Verify Email" icon="user-plus">

    Visit [https://app.coterie.rsvp/signup](https://app.coterie.rsvp/signup) and enter your email address.

    Click the verification link sent to your inbox. This secures your account and enables core features.

    <Image
      src="https://www.coterie.rsvp/images/signup-example.png"
      alt="Signup form with email field"
      width="600"
      height="400"
    />

  </Step>

  <Step title="Build Your First Circle" icon="users">

    From the dashboard, click **New Circle**.

    Enter a name like "Creator Support Group" and add a short description: "Mutual support for content growth."

    Customize settings:
    - Set session frequency (e.g., weekly)
    - Define reciprocity rules (e.g., comment on 3 posts per session)

    Click **Create Circle** to save.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite Members" icon="mail">

    In your circle dashboard, select **Invite Members**.

    Choose your method:

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Email Invites" icon="mail">

        Enter up to 10 email addresses separated by commas.

        ```

        john@example.com, jane@example.com, alex@example.com

        ```

        Send personalized invites with your circle details.

      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Shareable Link" icon="link">

        Generate a unique invite link:

        `https://app.coterie.rsvp/join/your-circle-id`

        Share it via social media, DMs, or your newsletter.

        Members sign up automatically upon joining.

      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch First Support Session" icon="zap">

    Navigate to your circle's **Sessions** tab.

    Click **Start New Session**.

    Review active tasks (e.g., "Support 3 posts from members").

    Mark tasks complete as you engage. Sessions auto-close after 24 hours.

    Monitor reciprocity metrics in real-time.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="success">

Congratulations! Your first Coterie is live. Watch engagement grow through structured reciprocity.

</Callout>
