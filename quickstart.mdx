There are two ways to get started with Coterie: **joining** an existing community, or **creating** your own. Pick the path that matches what you're doing.

<Callout kind="info">
  No credit card is required for either path — Coterie doesn't have live billing yet, so joining and running a community are both free today.
</Callout>

## Joining a Coterie

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a Coterie" icon="search">
    Browse public communities from the Discover page inside the app, or use the invite link a creator shared with you directly — something like [https://coterie.rsvp/c/their-community](https://coterie.rsvp/c/their-community).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Apply" icon="edit-3">
    Each Coterie has its own public page at `coterie.rsvp/c/:slug`. Fill in your name, email, and any questions the creator has added, then submit your application.

    Some communities auto-approve applications for instant access; others are reviewed by an admin first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
    Once approved, you'll get an invite to create your account at [https://app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp). Sign up with email and password, or continue with Google or GitHub.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete onboarding" icon="compass">
    Pick your niche and accept the community's rules. This is what determines whose posts you'll be matched with.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit your first post" icon="link">
    From your dashboard, submit a link to something you've published into the next open party. You'll then be assigned other members' posts to engage with — complete those first if you owe comment debt from a previous round.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="success">
  That's it — you're in the loop. Check your dashboard for your credit balance, reliability score, and upcoming sessions.
</Callout>

## Creating your own Coterie

Running a community means setting up niches, party rules, and branding before you invite anyone. The full walkthrough lives in [Create Your Coterie](/help-center/guides/create-your-coterie) — the short version:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account and community" icon="rocket">
    Sign up at [https://app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp) and create your Coterie.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure settings" icon="settings">
    Set your allowed content channels (LinkedIn, blog, Instagram, etc.), default party size, and comment-debt rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add niches" icon="layers">
    Define the niches members can join so assignments stay relevant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review applications" icon="check-circle">
    Approve or reject applicants as they come in through your public apply page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule your first party" icon="calendar">
    Open a session so members have somewhere to submit their first posts.
  </Step>
</Steps>
