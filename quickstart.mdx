There are two ways to get started with Coterie: **joining** an existing community, or **creating** your own. Pick the path that matches what you're doing — both have full guides linked below if you want more detail than the fast version here.

<Callout kind="info">
  No credit card required for either path — Coterie doesn't have live billing yet, so joining and running a community are both free today.
</Callout>

## Joining a Coterie

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a Coterie" icon="search">
    Browse public communities from Discover inside the app, or use the invite link a creator shared with you — something like [https://coterie.rsvp/c/their-community](https://coterie.rsvp/c/their-community).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply" icon="edit-3">
    Fill in your name, email, and any questions the creator's added at their public page (`coterie.rsvp/c/:slug`), then submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account" icon="user-plus">
    Once approved, sign up at [app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp) with the email you applied with — password, Google, or GitHub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboard and submit" icon="link">
    Pick your niche, then submit your first post into an open party.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See the full [Joining a Coterie](/members/joining) guide for details on applications, approval, and what happens next.

## Creating your own Coterie

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account and community" icon="rocket">
    Sign up at [app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp) and create your Coterie.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure settings" icon="settings">
    Set your allowed content channels, default party size, and comment-debt rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add niches" icon="layers">
    Define the niches members can join so assignments stay relevant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review applications and schedule a party" icon="calendar">
    Approve your first members and open a session so they have somewhere to submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See the full [Creating Your Coterie](/creators/creating-your-coterie) guide for the complete setup walkthrough, including branding and custom domains.
