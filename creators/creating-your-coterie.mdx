## Why run a Coterie

If you've built an audience — a niche following, a client base, a professional network — you already have the hardest part. What most creators are missing is the infrastructure to turn that audience into a community that actively works for its members, instead of a list that only hears from you when you post.

A Coterie gives your people a reason to show up regularly, not just a place to lurk. It's structured enough that engagement actually happens — no admin nagging people to comment on each other's posts — and it's yours: your niche, your rules, your brand.

## Before you start

Decide two things up front, because they shape everything else:

- **Who is this for?** The tighter the niche, the better the matching. "Marketers" is broad; "B2B SaaS marketers doing organic LinkedIn" is a Coterie people will actually feel is theirs.
- **How strict is the door?** Auto-approve gets you members faster; manual review gets you a higher bar. Neither is wrong — it depends on whether you're optimizing for growth or quality early on.

## Setting up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account and community" icon="rocket">
    Sign up at [app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp) — email and password, or Google or GitHub — then create your Coterie.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your settings" icon="sliders">
    From your admin dashboard, set your allowed content channels, default party size and time window, and comment-debt rules. See [Settings &amp; Party Rules](/creators/settings-and-party-rules) for the full breakdown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your niches" icon="layers">
    Define the niches members will pick from during onboarding — see [Managing Niches](/creators/managing-niches).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your branding" icon="palette">
    Add your logo and accent color. If you want your community to live on its own domain instead of an `app.coterie.rsvp` URL, see [Branding &amp; White-Label](/pro/branding-and-white-label).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share your apply page" icon="link">
    Every Coterie gets a public page at `coterie.rsvp/c/your-slug`. Decide whether applications need your review or should auto-approve — see [Reviewing Applications](/creators/reviewing-applications).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Schedule your first party" icon="calendar">
    Open a session so your first members have somewhere to submit their posts and get assigned engagement — see [Scheduling Parties](/creators/scheduling-parties).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start small. A dozen members who actually show up beats a hundred who signed up and never came back. You can always open the doors wider once your first party proves the loop works.
</Callout>
