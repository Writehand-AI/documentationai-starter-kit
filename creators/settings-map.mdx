## How the admin area is organised

Everything is reached from **Admin**, which is the single hub for running your Coterie. Within it, **Main settings** holds five tabs, ordered the way you'd actually set a Coterie up: what it looks like, how people find it, how they get in, how it runs, and what your public page says.

## Main settings

### Branding

Your identity. Account name and URL slug, brand name, tagline, logo, square logo icon, and accent colour.

<Callout kind="info">
  The **accent colour** here overrides the Primary colour on the Theme page. If you set a colour on Theme and it doesn't seem to apply, this is why — the accent wins.
</Callout>

### Discovery

Whether people can find you, and what they see when they do. **List in Discover** is the master switch: Public puts you in the directory, Private keeps you out of it entirely.

The rest fills in your directory card — cover image, About description, category, location, country, format, and cadence. Format and cadence also appear on your public Title Page if you've enabled its stats row.

### Joining & Access

How people get in: **Open join**, **Instant access**, and the questions on your apply page. This is also where paid membership requirements appear once you've set up plans. See [Joining & Access](/creators/joining-and-access) for how the switches interact.

### Sessions & Rules

How the community actually runs. Niche matching, allowed content channels, circle rotation, competitor exclusion, strike threshold, content policy, and session notification and reminder settings.

### Title Page

Your public page at `/c/your-slug` — headline, body, media gallery, footer links, and whether it's published. Unpublished, that URL falls back to a plain apply form.

## Elsewhere in Admin

| Where | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Applications** | Review, approve and decline people who applied |
| **Sessions** | Schedule sessions, group members into circles, Capacity Advisor |
| **Members** | Your roster — search, filter, see participation |
| **Coterie health** | Participation, retention, who's gone quiet |
| **Theme** | Fonts, corner radius, and the full colour palette including the sidebar |
| **Custom menu** | Your own links in the member sidebar |
| **Payments** | What your members pay **you** — plans and Stripe Connect |
| **Subscription** | What you pay **Coterie** — your Standard or Pro plan |
| **Reports** | Flagged posts and commenters |
| **API** and **Logs** | Keys, usage, and your audit trail |

### Pro only

**Forms**, **Staff access** (co-admins and moderators), **Assistant**, **Email** (your own SMTP and email wording), **White-label & Domain**, and **AI** (bring your own OpenAI key).

## Two things that catch people out

**Payments and Subscription are different things.** Payments is money flowing *to* you from your members. Subscription is money flowing *from* you to Coterie for the platform. They sit next to each other in Admin and the names are easy to swap.

**Branding, Theme and White-label all affect how things look.** Branding is your logo and accent colour. Theme is the full palette, fonts and corner radius. White-label (Pro) is favicon, page title and description, plus hiding Coterie's own navigation. If an appearance change isn't showing up, check whether another surface is overriding it.
