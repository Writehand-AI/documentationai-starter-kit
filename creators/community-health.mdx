## What it's for

Individual moderation catches individual problems. Community Health is where you catch the slower, quieter kind — engagement drifting down across the board, a niche going quiet, a party consistently under-filling. These are the leading indicators of churn, and they're much easier to act on before members actually start leaving than after.

## What to look at

- **Engagement trends** — are completion rates on assignments holding steady, or drifting down party over party?
- **Party fill rate** — are your sessions filling up, or running under-capacity? A consistently under-filled party is often a scheduling or cadence problem, not a member-quality one.
- **Churn-risk signals** — members whose reliability is trending down before they've hit a strike, which is your window to intervene early.

This view looks at your community in aggregate, not member-by-member — pair it with [moderation](/creators/moderation-and-reports) when a specific member needs individual attention.

<Callout kind="tip">
  A dip after a slow holiday week is normal. A steady multi-party decline usually means something structural — your cadence, your party size, or your niche definitions are worth revisiting.
</Callout>
