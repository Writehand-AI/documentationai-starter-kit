## Overview

Coterie is a members-only space for like-minded people built with Productised. 

- Curate your own community
- Host discussions, share updates
- Invite only your people — not everyone

## Core Idea

You don’t need everyone. You just need your people. That’s a Coterie.

## How do I join?

1. Receive an invite from an existing Coterie
2. Follow the invite link
3. Complete your member profile
