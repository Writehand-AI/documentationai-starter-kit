## Overview

Coterie is a platform for running give-to-get engagement communities. Members submit links to content they've published, get matched with other members' posts to comment on or give feedback on, and earn credit for showing up. It's structured reciprocity, not an open feed — engagement happens in scheduled sessions called **parties**.

- Curate your own invite-vetted community
- Match members by niche so feedback is relevant
- Keep reciprocity honest with credits and comment debt

## Core Idea

You don't need everyone. You just need your people. That's a Coterie.

## How do I join?

1. Apply through a Coterie's public page (`coterie.rsvp/c/:slug`), or an invite link from a member
2. Get approved by the community's admin (some communities auto-approve)
3. Create your account at [app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp) and complete onboarding

See the full [Quickstart](/quickstart) for a step-by-step walkthrough.

## How does engagement actually work?

Submit a post into an open party, and you'll be assigned other members' posts to engage with in return. Complete your assignments to earn credit — you can't submit a new post while you still owe engagement from a previous round.
