---
title: Joining Multiple Coteries
description: Can you be a member of more than one Coterie at once?
---

## Yes

You can belong to more than one Coterie at the same time — there's nothing stopping you from being a member of several communities across different niches.

## How they interact

Each Coterie you're in is independent: separate credit balance, separate reliability score, separate niche, separate parties. Completing assignments in one community has no effect on your standing in another.

## Switching between them

Once you're signed in at [app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp), you can switch between the communities you belong to from within the app.
