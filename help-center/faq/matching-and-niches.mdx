## The short version

When a party moves into grouping, Coterie automatically matches you with a set of other members' posts to review, matched primarily within your **niche** — the industry or content category you picked when you joined.

## Why niche, and not random

Random matching is what makes generic engagement pods feel useless — you end up reviewing things you don't understand. Niche matching keeps your matches relevant to what you actually know.

## Can my Coterie ignore niche?

Yes — a creator can set matching to **mixed**, which groups anyone with anyone. It's a reasonable choice while a community is still small, since a niche with four people in it can't fill a circle on its own.
