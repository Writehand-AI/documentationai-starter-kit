## The short version

When a party moves into grouping, Coterie automatically assigns you a set of other members' posts to engage with, matched primarily within your **niche** — the industry or content category you picked when you joined.

## Why niche, and not random

Random matching is what makes generic engagement pods feel useless — you end up commenting on things you don't understand. Niche matching keeps assignments relevant to what you actually know.

## What about competitors?

Some communities turn on competition-exclusion, which keeps direct competitors out of each other's assignment pool even within the same niche. Whether this is on depends on your Coterie's settings — ask your admin if you're not sure.
