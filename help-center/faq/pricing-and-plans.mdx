---
title: Pricing & Plans
description: Does Coterie cost anything, for members or creators?
---

## Is joining a Coterie free?

Yes. Applying to and being a member of a Coterie doesn't cost anything today.

## Is running a Coterie free?

Also yes, for now. Creators can set up and run a full community — including branding, custom domains, and niches — at no cost.

## Can creators charge members?

Creators can define paid plan tiers from [Plans &amp; Pricing](/pro/plans-and-pricing) — name, price, interval, and what's included. Live checkout isn't connected yet, so plans can be defined but not paid for through the app today.

<Callout kind="info">
  If a Coterie you're in lists paid tiers, treat them as the creator's stated pricing plan rather than something you can currently subscribe to inside the app.
</Callout>
