## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Coteries">
    Log in at `https://productised.com/coteries` and click "Create New Coterie".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your group">
    Enter the name and a short description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set privacy">
    Choose whether your Coterie is invite-only or request-to-join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite members">
    Add email addresses of your people and send invites.
  </Step>
</Steps>
