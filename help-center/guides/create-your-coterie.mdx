## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp) with email and password, or continue with Google or GitHub, then create your Coterie.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your settings">
    From your admin dashboard, set your allowed content channels (LinkedIn, blog, Instagram, and so on), default party size and time window, and comment-debt rules.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your niches">
    Define the niches members will pick from during onboarding, so assignments stay relevant to what your community actually cares about.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your branding">
    Add your logo and accent color under Branding. Custom domains are supported if you want your community to run under its own domain instead of an `app.coterie.rsvp` URL.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share your apply page">
    Every Coterie gets a public page at `coterie.rsvp/c/your-slug` where people can apply to join. Decide whether applications need your manual approval or should auto-approve for instant access.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review applications">
    As applications come in, approve or reject them from your Applications queue. Approved applicants get an invite to create their account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Schedule your first party">
    Open a session from Parties so your first members have somewhere to submit their posts and get assigned engagement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start small. A handful of engaged members giving real feedback beats a large group that never shows up.
</Callout>
