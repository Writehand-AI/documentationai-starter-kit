---
title: Custom Domain Not Working
description: For creators — fixing a custom domain that isn't resolving or verifying.
---

## Symptoms

- Your domain doesn't load your Coterie
- Domain settings show it as unverified after setup

## Causes and fixes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Double-check your DNS records">
    Make sure the records you added at your DNS provider exactly match what's shown in your Domain settings — a typo in a single character will block verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it time to propagate">
    DNS changes aren't instant. Propagation can take anywhere from a few minutes to a couple of days depending on your provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recheck verification">
    Once you're confident the records are correct and enough time has passed, re-check your domain's status from Domain settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Custom Domains](/pro/custom-domains) for the full setup walkthrough.
