---
title: No Party to Join
description: What to do when there's nothing open to submit into.
---

## Symptoms

- Submit is unavailable and there's no comment debt blocking it
- Sessions calendar shows nothing upcoming

## Causes

- Your Coterie's admin hasn't scheduled a new party yet
- A party is scheduled but hasn't opened for submissions
- You haven't opted in to an upcoming session

## Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Sessions">
    Look at your Sessions calendar for the next scheduled party and its opening time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Opt in if needed">
    Some communities require you to opt in to a party ahead of time — do that if you see an upcoming one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your admin">
    If nothing's scheduled at all, your community's admin controls cadence — ask them when the next one's planned.
  </Step>
</Steps>
