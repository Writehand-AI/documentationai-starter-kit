---
title: Can't Submit a Post
description: Why Submit might be blocked, and how to clear it.
---

## Symptoms

- Submit is greyed out or blocked
- A message about outstanding reviews

## Cause

Unfinished reviews from a previous party don't block you from submitting to a new one. What decides that is whether you've joined the session and it's still open. Falling behind costs you reliability and the contribution you'd have earned, and your owner can see it — but it isn't a lockout. See [Recognition &amp; Reliability](/members/recognition-and-reliability) for why.

## Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Assignments">
    Open your Assignments tab and see what's still outstanding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete or skip them">
    Review each matched post and mark it done. If you genuinely can't get to one, skip it with a reason rather than letting it sit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try submitting again">
    Once your outstanding reviews clear, Submit unlocks automatically — no need to contact anyone.
  </Step>
</Steps>
