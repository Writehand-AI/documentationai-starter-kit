---
title: Can't Submit a Post
description: Why Submit might be blocked, and how to clear it.
---

## Symptoms

- Submit is greyed out or blocked
- A message about outstanding assignments or "comment debt"

## Cause

You have unfinished assignments from a previous party. Coterie blocks new submissions until you've caught up — this is intentional, not a bug. See [Credits &amp; Reliability](/members/credits-and-reliability) for why.

## Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Assignments">
    Open your Assignments tab and see what's still outstanding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete or skip them">
    Engage with each assigned post and mark it done. If you genuinely can't get to one, skip it with a reason rather than letting it sit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try submitting again">
    Once your debt clears, Submit unlocks automatically — no need to contact anyone.
  </Step>
</Steps>
