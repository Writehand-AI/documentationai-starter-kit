---
title: Cannot Join a Coterie
description: Resolve join issues quickly.
---

## Symptoms

- Applying at a Coterie's page (`coterie.rsvp/c/:slug`) doesn't lead anywhere, or shows an error
- You applied but never got an invite
- You have an invite, but signing up at `app.coterie.rsvp` fails
- You're signed in but don't see the community you expected

## Causes and fixes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the link">
    Make sure you're using the exact link the creator shared, or the one from their public apply page. A mistyped slug will 404 instead of loading the community.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your application may still be pending">
    Not every Coterie auto-approves. If the creator reviews applications manually, you'll need to wait for approval before you get an invite — check back with them if it's been a while.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your account with the invited email">
    Your invite is tied to the email address you applied with. Sign up at [app.coterie.rsvp](https://app.coterie.rsvp) using that same address — a mismatch will block account creation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Even after your account exists, you won't see the community's dashboard until you finish onboarding (picking a niche and accepting the rules).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask the admin to resend">
    If you're still stuck, ask the Coterie's admin to check your application status or resend your invite from their applications queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>
