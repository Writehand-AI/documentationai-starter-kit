---
title: Cannot Join a Coterie
description: Resolve join issues quickly.
---

## Symptoms

- Invite link doesn’t work
- Error: "Access denied" or "Link expired"

## Causes

- Invite link used or expired
- Not logged in to Productised

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check invite">
    Ensure the invite link is valid and unused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Login">
    Login to your Productised account before using the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact owner">
    Ask the Coterie admin to resend your invite if still stuck.
  </Step>
</Steps>
